About Us

DUFF BEER started as a fun idea between a couple of friends who shared a love for creative design, pop culture, and building something together. What began as a small project quickly turned into a fictional business concept where we could combine our ideas and create a brand that felt bold, memorable, and fun.

Each friend brought something different to the table. We worked together on the look of the brand, the website, and the ideas behind the business. Along the way, we learned that a good business is not just about having a cool idea; it is also about teamwork, creativity, and being willing to keep improving.

Today, this site represents what can happen when a couple of friends take an idea and turn it into a project they can be proud of. DUFF BEER is a fictional, fan-made web design concept inspired by The Simpsons.

This is a fictional fan-site concept and does not sell or distribute alcohol.
